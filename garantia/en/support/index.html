Help with the Garantia app

Have a question or a problem with Garantia? Email umaiya.app@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Do not send a receipt or serial number unless we ask for it.

Back up or transfer your data

In Garantia Settings, create a ZIP backup and choose a location from the iOS share sheet, such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive. To restore, select the ZIP file using the import function. Keep a safe copy of the file: Garantia does not store a cloud copy.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Garantia in iOS Settings. iOS limits pending notifications; the app schedules the nearest warranty dates first.

Garantia Plus

Purchases are managed by the App Store. If Plus is not recognized after a purchase, open the Garantia Plus screen and use “Restore Purchases” with the same Apple Account.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy to learn how local storage, OCR, notifications, and exports work.
