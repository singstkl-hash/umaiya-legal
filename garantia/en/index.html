GARANTIA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Garantia keeps your inventory and supporting documents in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not create an account or send your records, photos, or documents to a server operated by Umaiya Studio.

Controller and contact

Garantia is published by Umaiya Studio (SIREN 109 200 568, France). For questions about this policy or your data, contact umaiya.app@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Depending on what you enter, Garantia stores item names and categories, warranty dates and durations, prices, sellers, references and serial numbers, notes, preferences, and attached photos and files locally. This information is used to show your inventory and manage warranty dates.

You can delete items and their attachments in the app. Deleting the app removes data from its local storage according to the operating system’s behavior, but does not delete copies you exported or any device backups managed by Apple.

Camera and text recognition

If you choose to photograph a receipt or other document, the image is saved as an attachment on your device. Text recognition (OCR) runs on the device to suggest form fields. Recognized text is not sent to Umaiya Studio. You can review and edit suggested fields.

Notifications and biometrics

With your permission, Garantia schedules warranty reminders as local notifications on your device. The app does not use a remote push-notification service for these reminders.

If you enable the Plus app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is checked by iOS. Garantia receives only the authentication result, never your biometric data, which remains under Apple’s system controls.

In-app purchases

Purchasing or restoring Garantia Plus uses the App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and provides the app with purchase status. Umaiya Studio does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account credentials.

Exports and file providers

A ZIP backup is created locally. When you choose to share or save it to Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or another provider offered by iOS, you send the file to that service by your choice. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms then apply. Garantia does not automatically sync data between devices.

Data sharing, advertising, and analytics

Garantia has no user account, advertising, audience analytics tool, or Garantia server for storing your inventory. Apart from communications needed by the App Store for purchases and any sharing action you initiate, your item records and documents remain in the app’s local storage.

Your choices and rights

You can manage or delete your records in the app, turn off notifications in iOS Settings, and choose not to share files. For requests about your rights under applicable data-protection laws, email umaiya.app@gmail.com. You may also contact the French data-protection authority, CNIL.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if Garantia’s practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
