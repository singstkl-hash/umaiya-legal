Privacy Policy — Umaiya Universe

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Umaiya Universe does not collect any personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

Publisher

Umaiya Studio (SIREN 109 200 568), publisher of the Umaiya Universe app for iPhone and iPad.

Data collected

None. The app does not require an account, does not send any information to a server and includes no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Data stored on your device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app saves the following locally, on your device only:

  • your progress through chapters and games;
  • your review notebook and favorite words;
  • your preferences (interface language, romaji help, mood).

This data never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you back up your device (iCloud or a computer), it may be included in that backup, depending on your own Apple settings.

Microphone

The app never turns on the microphone and does not record your voice. The mention shown in iOS settings comes from the audio library used to play sounds.

Advertising and tracking

No ads. No advertising identifier is used. No tracking across apps or websites.

Children

Since the app collects no data at all, it does not collect any data from children either.

Purchases

The current version offers no purchases. If purchases are added later, they will be handled by Apple, and this page will be updated before they go live.

Your rights

Since the publisher holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For any question:

Contact: umaiya.app@gmail.com

Please put “Umaiya Universe” in the subject line, along with your device model and iOS version. We reply within a few business days.

Changes

This policy may change along with the app. The date at the top of the page shows the latest update.
