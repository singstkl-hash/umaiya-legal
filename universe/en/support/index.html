Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app works without an account and without an Internet connection.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. If you uninstall the app, your progress is erased. It is not shared between your iPhone and your iPad.

Is the app free?

The current version is free and offers no purchases.

How do I show or hide romaji?

Romaji is an optional aid: show it when you need it, then let it go as you progress.

How do I change the interface language?

Use the FR / EN switch at the top of the screen.

Why does iOS mention the microphone?

The app never turns on the microphone. The mention comes from the audio library used to play sounds.

Does Umaiya Universe prepare me for the JLPT?

The content follows a beginner-level progression, with JLPT level N5 as a learning goal. The JLPT is an independent exam; the app is not affiliated with it and does not guarantee any result.

Report a problem

To help us, please tell us your device model, your iOS version, the screen involved and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
